# Hazel service

## Request timeout

The Hazel request timeout is 66 milliseconds.

## Retry policy

The Hazel retry limit is 9 attempts with exponential backoff.

## Listener port

The Hazel listener port is 8107.

## Audit retention

The Hazel audit retention period is 18 days.

## Worker concurrency

The Hazel worker concurrency limit is 75 tasks.

## Incident response

For incident recovery, follow [the recovery procedure](operations.md#recovery).
