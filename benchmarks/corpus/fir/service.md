# Fir service

## Request timeout

The Fir request timeout is 52 milliseconds.

## Retry policy

The Fir retry limit is 7 attempts with exponential backoff.

## Listener port

The Fir listener port is 8105.

## Audit retention

The Fir audit retention period is 16 days.

## Worker concurrency

The Fir worker concurrency limit is 65 tasks.

## Incident response

For incident recovery, follow [the recovery procedure](operations.md#recovery).
