# Cedar service

## Request timeout

The Cedar request timeout is 31 milliseconds.

## Retry policy

The Cedar retry limit is 4 attempts with exponential backoff.

## Listener port

The Cedar listener port is 8102.

## Audit retention

The Cedar audit retention period is 13 days.

## Worker concurrency

The Cedar worker concurrency limit is 50 tasks.

## Incident response

For incident recovery, follow [the recovery procedure](operations.md#recovery).
