# Amber service

## Request timeout

The Amber request timeout is 17 milliseconds.

## Retry policy

The Amber retry limit is 2 attempts with exponential backoff.

## Listener port

The Amber listener port is 8100.

## Audit retention

The Amber audit retention period is 11 days.

## Worker concurrency

The Amber worker concurrency limit is 40 tasks.

## Incident response

For incident recovery, follow [the recovery procedure](operations.md#recovery).
