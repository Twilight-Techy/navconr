# Juniper service

## Request timeout

The Juniper request timeout is 80 milliseconds.

## Retry policy

The Juniper retry limit is 11 attempts with exponential backoff.

## Listener port

The Juniper listener port is 8109.

## Audit retention

The Juniper audit retention period is 20 days.

## Worker concurrency

The Juniper worker concurrency limit is 85 tasks.

## Incident response

For incident recovery, follow [the recovery procedure](operations.md#recovery).
