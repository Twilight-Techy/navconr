# Birch service

## Request timeout

The Birch request timeout is 24 milliseconds.

## Retry policy

The Birch retry limit is 3 attempts with exponential backoff.

## Listener port

The Birch listener port is 8101.

## Audit retention

The Birch audit retention period is 12 days.

## Worker concurrency

The Birch worker concurrency limit is 45 tasks.

## Incident response

For incident recovery, follow [the recovery procedure](operations.md#recovery).
