# Iris service

## Request timeout

The Iris request timeout is 73 milliseconds.

## Retry policy

The Iris retry limit is 10 attempts with exponential backoff.

## Listener port

The Iris listener port is 8108.

## Audit retention

The Iris audit retention period is 19 days.

## Worker concurrency

The Iris worker concurrency limit is 80 tasks.

## Incident response

For incident recovery, follow [the recovery procedure](operations.md#recovery).
