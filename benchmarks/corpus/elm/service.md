# Elm service

## Request timeout

The Elm request timeout is 45 milliseconds.

## Retry policy

The Elm retry limit is 6 attempts with exponential backoff.

## Listener port

The Elm listener port is 8104.

## Audit retention

The Elm audit retention period is 15 days.

## Worker concurrency

The Elm worker concurrency limit is 60 tasks.

## Incident response

For incident recovery, follow [the recovery procedure](operations.md#recovery).
