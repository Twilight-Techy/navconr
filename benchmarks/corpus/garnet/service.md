# Garnet service

## Request timeout

The Garnet request timeout is 59 milliseconds.

## Retry policy

The Garnet retry limit is 8 attempts with exponential backoff.

## Listener port

The Garnet listener port is 8106.

## Audit retention

The Garnet audit retention period is 17 days.

## Worker concurrency

The Garnet worker concurrency limit is 70 tasks.

## Incident response

For incident recovery, follow [the recovery procedure](operations.md#recovery).
